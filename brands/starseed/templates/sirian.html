Sirian Starseeds: Attributes and Description

Sirian Starseeds are souls that have originated from or spent significant lifetimes in the Sirius star system, primarily Sirius A and Sirius B. These beings carry deep spiritual wisdom, ancient knowledge, and a strong connection to both cosmic and earthly energies.

Many Sirian Starseeds played vital roles in ancient civilizations like Atlantis, Lemuria, and Egypt, contributing their advanced technological, spiritual, and healing knowledge.
